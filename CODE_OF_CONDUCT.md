# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a constructive, welcoming, and professional project community.

Be respectful, focus criticism on ideas and code, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, protect private information, and assume good faith while still holding changes to high technical standards.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Serious concerns may be reported privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`.
