# Code of Conduct

DocMergeForge aims to maintain a constructive, welcoming, technically rigorous, and privacy-conscious open-source community.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- treat people with respect regardless of experience level, background, identity, location, or communication style;
- focus technical criticism on code, behavior, evidence, and design rather than on the person proposing a change;
- give reproducible feedback and accept correction when evidence changes;
- protect private manuscripts, local paths, credentials, passwords, diagnostics, and other sensitive material;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they materially affect a review or recommendation;
- respect project safety boundaries, especially source-file immutability and companion-code separation; and
- avoid presenting unverified features, external-office fidelity, unsigned artifacts, or incomplete acceptance work as production-ready.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, or targeted personal attacks;
- sexualized conduct or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- intentionally posting secrets, passwords, confidential manuscripts, private recovery artifacts, or other sensitive data;
- sustained disruption, spam, malicious issue filing, or knowingly false technical claims;
- pressuring contributors to bypass tests, security review, licensing obligations, or documented safety gates; or
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, project-maintained community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing DocMergeForge or acting in an official project role.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject or close contributions, limit participation, lock discussions, or take other proportionate action when behavior violates these expectations. Enforcement should focus on protecting participants and the project rather than escalating conflict.

For ordinary project support, use the guidance in [`docs/support.md`](docs/support.md). Security-sensitive reports must follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and should not be posted publicly.

For a private conduct concern, contact `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Include only the information needed to understand the concern, and do not attach unrelated private manuscript data or credentials.
